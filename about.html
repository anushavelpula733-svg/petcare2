About Us

Anu PetCare & Veterinary Clinic is dedicated to providing compassionate and professional healthcare for pets. Our clinic provides quality treatment, vaccination, grooming and preventive care.

Our Mission 🐾

Our mission is to provide safe, affordable and high-quality veterinary care for every pet. We believe every animal deserves love, protection and a healthy life.

Our Vision ❤️

Our vision is to create a healthier and happier community for pets by combining modern veterinary care with kindness, trust and responsible pet ownership.
